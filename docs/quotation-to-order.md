# From Quotation to Sales Order

1. Create the quotation from the opportunity (state **draft**).
2. Send it to the customer by email (state **sent**).
3. When the customer accepts, click **Confirm** to turn it into a sales order (state **sale**).
4. Cancelled quotations or orders move to state **cancel** and cannot be reopened; create a new quotation instead.

Quotations are valid for **30 days** by default. Orders above 10,000 EUR require a signed purchase order from the customer before confirmation. Confirming an order moves the linked opportunity to the **Won** stage.
