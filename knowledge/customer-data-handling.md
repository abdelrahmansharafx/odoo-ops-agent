# Customer Data Handling

- Only store business contact details needed for the sale: name, work email, work phone, company, city and country.
- Never store payment card numbers, passwords or national ID numbers in Odoo.
- Customers may request deletion of their personal data; sales operations must action it within **30 days**.
- Exports of customer lists require sales manager approval and must not be sent over personal email.
- Data in the demo and training environments is synthetic and must never be mixed with production data.
