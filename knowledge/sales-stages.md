# Sales Pipeline Stages

Every opportunity sits in exactly one stage.

| Stage | Meaning | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **New** | Lead captured, not yet contacted | First contact attempt made |
| **Qualified** | Budget, need and decision-maker confirmed | Discovery call completed |
| **Proposition** | A quotation has been sent | Customer accepts or rejects the quote |
| **Won** | Quotation confirmed as a sales order | None (closed) |

An opportunity may be marked **Lost** from any stage; a lost reason is mandatory. Probability defaults: New 10%, Qualified 30%, Proposition 60%, Won 100%.
