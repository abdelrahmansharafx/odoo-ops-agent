# Discount Approval Matrix

| Discount on list price | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Salesperson, no approval needed |
| 10% to 20% | Sales manager |
| Above 20% | Sales director |

Discounts apply to the whole quotation, never to individual lines in isolation. A discount approval is valid for **14 days**; after that the quotation must be re-approved. Multi-year contracts may receive an extra 5% with director approval.
