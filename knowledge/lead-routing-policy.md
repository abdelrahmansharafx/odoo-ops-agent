# Lead Routing Policy

New leads are assigned within **2 business hours** of creation.

## Rules
- Leads from companies with more than 250 employees go to the **Enterprise team**.
- Leads from companies with 250 employees or fewer go to the **SMB team**, assigned round-robin.
- Leads from an existing customer go to that customer's current account owner, regardless of size.
- Leads with no company name are held in the **New** stage for manual triage by the sales operations lead.

## Escalation
If a lead has no salesperson after 2 business hours, sales operations reassigns it and notes the reason in the chatter.
