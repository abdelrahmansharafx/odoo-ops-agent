# Refund Policy

- Customers may request a full refund within **30 days** of the invoice date.
- Refunds between day 31 and day 60 are issued as **credit notes** only, not cash.
- No refunds after 60 days, except for proven product defects (handled by support).

## Approval
- Refunds up to **500 EUR**: sales representative approves.
- Refunds above **500 EUR**: sales manager must approve.
- Refunds above **5,000 EUR**: finance director must also approve.

Refunds are processed within **10 business days** of approval.
